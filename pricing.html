Fair question

Can’t I just use AI?

For the writing, partly. Use it. That’s what Bid Check is for.

AI won’t tell you which tenders are worth your time. It won’t know a buyer wants three years of NHS work before you’ve spent a weekend writing. It doesn’t know how the answers get scored, and it won’t spot the missing policy that gets a bid thrown out.

That’s the bit we do. You do the work. We do the paperwork.
