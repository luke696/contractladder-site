Questions

Before you ask

We've never done council work. Is that a problem?

No. Start with smaller quotations and lots, where track record matters less. The Radar tells you which ones are within reach and what you'd need for the bigger ones.

What do I need to have in place?

Usually employer's and public liability insurance, a health and safety policy, and two or three past jobs we can describe. Accreditations like CHAS or SafeContractor help. If you're missing something, we'll tell you what and why.

How quickly can you turn a bid round?

Quotations in 3 working days, full tenders in 7, counted from when we have your documents. Send the pack as early as you can.

Do you take a cut if we win?

No. You pay the fixed price and keep every penny of the contract.

Where do you cover?

Derbyshire, Nottinghamshire, Leicestershire, Lincolnshire, Northamptonshire and Rutland to start.
